# ShopMate General FAQ

## Membership tiers
- **Regular:** the default tier for all customers.
- **Premium:** paid tier with free express shipping, free return shipping, and an
  extended electronics warranty (15 months instead of 12).

## Order cancellation
- Orders can be **cancelled for free before they ship**, with a full refund to the
  original payment method.
- After an order ships, it can no longer be cancelled; instead, you can refuse
  delivery or start a return once it arrives.

## Payment methods
- We accept credit cards, debit cards, UPI, net banking, and ShopMate wallet.
- For security, the assistant never shows full card numbers — only the last 4
  digits (for example, "card ending 4242").

## Account and privacy
- You can update your saved addresses and contact details from your account.
- We do not share your personal information with third parties for marketing.

## Contacting a human specialist
- Most issues are resolved directly by the assistant.
- For complex cases (legal questions, disputes the assistant cannot resolve, or an
  explicit request), the assistant will escalate to a human specialist with a full
  summary so you never have to repeat yourself.

## What the assistant can do for you
- Check order status, check refund eligibility, issue refunds, cancel orders,
  reschedule delivery, update your address, track shipments, and create support
  tickets — and it confirms with you before any action that moves money.
