# ShopMate Warranty Terms

## Warranty coverage by category
- **Electronics:** **12 months** manufacturer warranty from the delivery date.
- **Accessories** (cables, cases, chargers): **6 months** warranty.
- **Apparel, home goods, and perishables:** no warranty; covered only by the
  return/refund windows.

## What the warranty covers
- Manufacturing defects and hardware failures under normal use.
- The warranty does **not** cover accidental damage, water damage, unauthorized
  repairs, or normal wear and tear.

## How warranty claims work
1. Report the issue within the warranty period.
2. Provide the order number and a description (and photos, if a physical defect).
3. Eligible claims are resolved by **repair, replacement, or refund**, in that
   order of preference, depending on stock and the nature of the defect.

## Warranty vs. refund
- A refund window (15 days for electronics) is about changing your mind or early
  defects; the **warranty** (12 months for electronics) covers defects that appear
  later during normal use.
- After the refund window closes, a defective electronic item is handled as a
  **warranty claim**, not a refund.

## Extended warranty
- Premium members receive an additional **3 months** of warranty on electronics
  (15 months total).
