# ShopMate Shipping FAQ

## Delivery times
- **Standard shipping:** 3–5 business days.
- **Express shipping:** 1–2 business days.
- Delivery estimates begin once the order is **shipped**, not when it is placed.

## Shipping charges
- **Free standard shipping** on orders over **₹999**.
- Orders under ₹999 have a flat standard shipping fee of **₹49**.
- Express shipping is **₹149**, or free for **Premium members**.

## Tracking your order
- A tracking link is emailed when your order ships.
- Order status moves through: **placed → shipped → delivered**.
- You can ask the assistant to check your order status at any time.

## Delayed orders
- If an order hasn't arrived within **2 business days after** the estimated delivery
  date, it is considered delayed and we can investigate or reship.
- For a confirmed lost shipment, you are entitled to a **free reshipment or a full
  refund**, your choice.

## Changing the delivery address
- You can change the delivery address **before the order is shipped**.
- After shipment, the address can no longer be changed, but delivery can sometimes
  be rescheduled with the courier.

## Rescheduling delivery
- Deliveries can be rescheduled to a later date while the order is in transit,
  subject to courier availability.

## Where we ship
- ShopMate currently ships within India. International shipping is not yet
  available.
