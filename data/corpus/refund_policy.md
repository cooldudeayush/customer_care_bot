# ShopMate Refund Policy

## Refund eligibility windows
- **Standard items:** You can request a refund within **30 days of delivery**.
- **Electronics:** Refunds are available within **15 days of delivery** because of
  hygiene, activation, and warranty considerations.
- **Perishables, gift cards, and final-sale items:** These are **non-refundable**
  and cannot be returned unless they arrived damaged or defective.

## How a refund is issued
- Refunds are returned to the **original payment method** used for the order.
- If the original method is unavailable (for example, an expired card), the refund
  is issued to your **ShopMate wallet** as store credit.
- We never issue refunds to a different person's account or card.

## Refund processing time
- Once a refund is approved, it is processed within **3–5 business days**.
- Your bank or card issuer may take an additional 2–7 business days to show the
  credit on your statement.

## Refund amount
- Eligible refunds return the **full item price**.
- Original shipping charges are refunded only if the item was **defective, damaged,
  or incorrect**. For change-of-mind refunds, original shipping is non-refundable.

## Duplicate or double charges
- If you were **charged twice** for the same order, the duplicate charge is fully
  refundable regardless of the item category, and is **not** counted against the
  refund window. Report it and we will reverse the duplicate charge.

## Partial refunds
- Partial refunds may apply to items returned in used or incomplete condition
  (for example, missing accessories or original packaging).
